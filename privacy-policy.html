Legal & Privacy Standards

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026 | Effective immediately for all visitors and patrons of quarterhosiery.com

1. Introduction & Overview

QuarterHosiery ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website located at quarterhosiery.com. We are committed to safeguarding your personal privacy and maintaining strict confidentiality regarding any personally identifiable data collected during your interaction with our online store, digital lookbooks, or contact inquiries.

2. Information We Collect

When you visit QuarterHosiery, we may collect information in two categories:

  • Voluntarily Provided Information: When you subscribe to our Technical Dispatch, submit a concierge inquiry through our contact form, or request sizing recommendations, you may provide your name, email address, postal address, and telephone number.
  • Automatically Logged Technical Information: Our web servers and analytical tags log standard HTTP request metadata, including your IP address, browser user-agent string, referring URLs, device display parameters, and session interaction timestamps.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis of Data Processing

We process collected data exclusively for lawful business purposes, including:

  • Fulfilling inquiries and providing technical fit recommendations for our quarter socks.
  • Delivering requested email newsletters and product availability notifications.
  • Optimizing website responsiveness, load speeds, and server performance.
  • Protecting against malicious bot activity, fraudulent transactions, and server intrusions.

4. Data Retention Protocols

We retain contact inquiry correspondence only for as long as necessary to address your specific support request or satisfy applicable statutory tax and accounting regulations. Marketing email subscriptions are retained until you elect to unsubscribe via the one-click link provided in every dispatch.

5. Third-Party Disclosures

QuarterHosiery does not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal information to commercial advertising networks or data brokers. We only share data with essential technical service providers (such as secure cloud hosting and email delivery infrastructure) bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable state, federal, and international privacy laws (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal records. To exercise these rights, submit a written request to concierge@quarterhosiery.com or visit our headquarters at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012.
