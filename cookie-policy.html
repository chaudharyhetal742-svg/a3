Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026 | Effective immediately for all visitors and patrons of quarterhosiery.com

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small alphanumeric text files placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile smartphone when you navigate websites. They allow servers to recognize returning sessions, preserve user preferences, and provide anonymous performance telemetry.

2. How QuarterHosiery Utilizes Cookies

Our website utilizes cookies for limited, functional purposes:

  • Essential Operational Cookies: Necessary to enable secure navigation, maintain shopping bag states, and prevent cross-site request forgery attacks.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: We utilize Google Analytics (via tag ID G-0LY0HY7L01) to gather anonymous, aggregated data on page view volumes, traffic sources, bounce rates, and session durations. No personally identifiable medical or financial data is transmitted.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your selected display preferences or sizing unit toggles (e.g., US vs. EU sizing).

3. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You have full control over cookie permissions. Most modern web browsers allow you to block, refuse, or delete cookies via your browser preference settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact certain interactive capabilities of this site.

4. Policy Updates

We may revise this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect technological adjustments or regulatory standards. Updates take effect immediately upon publication.
