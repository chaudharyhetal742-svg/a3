Our Story & Standards

Engineered at the Intersection of Podiatry and Knitwear

Founded on Mercer Street in New York, QuarterHosiery was born from a singular obsession: designing the world's most anatomically precise quarter-height sock.

Founding Mission

The Problem with Ordinary Socks

For decades, sock manufacturing prioritized high-speed mass production over foot anatomy. Traditional athletic socks relied on coarse 96-needle cylinders, abrasive machine-overlocked toe seams, and uniform elastic tubes that slip during vigorous movement.

We recognized that the supramalleolar ankle junction is one of the most vulnerable anatomical regions in active movement. Low-cut shoes constantly rub against the sensitive Achilles tendon, while tall crew socks trap excess thermal humidity.

QuarterHosiery engineered the solution: a dedicated 2.5-to-3-inch height profile powered by 200-needle circular knitting technology, graduated plantar arch stabilization, and hand-linked seamless closures.

Core Principles

Our Technical Commitments

Every pair bearing the QuarterHosiery mark satisfies our five fundamental manufacturing criteria.

200-Needle High Gauge

We refuse coarse knitting. Every sock is woven with 200 micro-needles, yielding an exceptionally smooth surface, tight abrasion resistance, and conformal anatomical fit.

Hand-Linked Seamless Toes

Zero ridge seams. We link the toe box loop-by-loop to prevent digital friction, blisters, and nail trauma during high-mileage runs and all-day walking.

Traceable Natural Fibers

We source certified organic combed cotton and ethically farmed, non-mulesed New Zealand merino wool certified under Oeko-Tex Standard 100.
