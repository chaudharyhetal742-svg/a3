2. No Medical or Podiatric Warranty

While our quarter socks are engineered with arch compression bands and cushioning pads designed to alleviate everyday walking fatigue and reduce skin friction, they are not regulated medical devices or orthopedic braces. Individuals suffering from severe plantar fasciitis, chronic peripheral neuropathy, acute Achilles tendinopathy, or circulatory disorders should consult a licensed physician or podiatrist before undertaking athletic training or using compression apparel.

3. Wear-and-Tear Disclosure

Sock longevity varies substantially based on individual foot biomechanics, runner gait patterns, terrain roughness, footwear internal lining condition, and laundry maintenance habits. Mechanical Martindale testing metrics represent controlled laboratory thresholds and do not guarantee infinite operational lifespan under aggressive outdoor field use.
